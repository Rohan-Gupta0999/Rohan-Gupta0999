## Key Project

### 1) Algora — Government Financial Transparency Platform
[View Repo] : https://github.com/Rohan-Gupta0999/Algora

**Problem Statement** (Corruption)
- Lack of transparent systems to track government fund allocation and usage  
- High risk of corruption and money laundering due to centralized control  
- Limited visibility for citizens into how public funds are utilized  

**Solution**
- Developed a blockchain-based platform using the Algorand network to ensure transparent and traceable fund flow  
- Introduced a tokenized system (ALGR) representing government funds for controlled digital transactions  
- Implemented multisignature authorization to prevent unilateral fund transfers by a single official  
- Ensured that officials do not directly control liquid cash, reducing misuse risk  
- Maintained all transactions on an immutable public ledger for auditability and trust  
- Enabled citizens to track transactions, including sender, receiver, amount, timestamp, and project details  
- Integrated a structured flow where banks are involved only at the final stage for converting tokens into fiat currency
